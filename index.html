About C-BROS

Built to Serve. Built to Last.

C-BROS CONSTRUCTION is based in Pinaripad Sur, Aglipay, Quirino, Philippines.

We undertake construction and infrastructure projects including buildings, roads, bridges, civil works, flood mitigation and rehabilitation and maintenance works.

Our project portfolio includes road improvements, bridge works, flood control structures and public facilities.
